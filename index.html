MowWhen
UK Lawn Mowing Advisor

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

MowWhen does not collect, store, or share any personal data. No account is required. Nothing is tracked.

What MowWhen does

MowWhen is a UK lawn mowing advisor. It uses weather forecast data from the Met Office to calculate a mowing suitability score for your location, helping you choose the best time to mow your lawn.

Location data

MowWhen requests access to your device's location for one purpose only: to fetch a local weather forecast from the Met Office.

  • Your latitude and longitude are sent to our secure server to retrieve a forecast.
  • Your location is not stored on our servers beyond the duration of the request.
  • Your location is not linked to any identifier, account, or profile.
  • Your location is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than fetching a weather forecast.

If you prefer not to grant location permission, you can instead enter a UK postcode manually. Postcode lookups are processed by postcodes.io, a free open-source service. No postcode data is stored by MowWhen.

Weather data

Weather forecasts are provided by the Met Office via the Weather DataHub API. Your location coordinates are forwarded to this service to retrieve a forecast. The Met Office's own privacy policy applies to this data transfer. MowWhen does not control how the Met Office handles API requests.

Forecast responses are cached on our server for up to 55 minutes to reduce unnecessary API calls. Cached data contains only weather information — no location coordinates or user identifiers are stored alongside it.

App preferences

MowWhen stores the following preferences locally on your device only, using your device's own secure storage:

  • Your saved location (postcode and place name)
  • Your last mow date
  • Your grass type preference

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to our servers, not backed up remotely, and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Analytics and tracking

MowWhen does not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking tools. There are no third-party SDKs in the app that collect behavioural data.

Children's privacy

MowWhen does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. The app contains no content directed at children and collects no personal information from any user.

Third-party services

MowWhen uses the following third-party services:

  • Met Office Weather DataHub — weather forecast data. Location coordinates are sent to this service. See Met Office Privacy Policy.
  • postcodes.io — UK postcode to coordinates lookup (only used if you search by postcode). See postcodes.io.
  • Supabase — our server infrastructure, used to proxy weather API requests. No personal data is stored. See Supabase Privacy Policy.

Data security

All communication between the app and our server uses HTTPS encryption. The Met Office API key is stored securely on our server and is never included in the app itself. App preferences stored on your device use your device's own secure local storage.

Your rights

Because MowWhen does not collect or store any personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy
